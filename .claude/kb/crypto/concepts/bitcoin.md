# Bitcoin Fundamentals

## What is Bitcoin

Bitcoin (BTC) is a decentralized digital currency created in 2009 by Satoshi Nakamoto.
It operates on a peer-to-peer network secured by cryptographic proof-of-work (PoW).
As of 2026, Bitcoin is the dominant reserve asset of the cryptocurrency market,
with its role defined by scarcity, liquidity, and institutional participation.

## Halving Cycle

Bitcoin undergoes a halving every 210,000 blocks (~4 years), cutting mining rewards in half.
This creates predictable supply shocks historically correlated with bull markets.

| Halving | Date | Reward | Price Before | Price Peak (after ~12mo) |
|---------|------|--------|-------------|--------------------------|
| 1st | Nov 2012 | 25 BTC | ~$12 | ~$1,100 |
| 2nd | Jul 2016 | 12.5 BTC | ~$650 | ~$20,000 |
| 3rd | May 2020 | 6.25 BTC | ~$8,500 | ~$69,000 |
| 4th | Apr 2024 | 3.125 BTC | ~$64,000 | TBD |

Post-halving bull markets typically peak 12-18 months after the event.

## Network Security Metrics

**Hash Rate:** Total computational power securing the Bitcoin network.
- Rising hash rate = more miners, stronger security, long-term bullish signal
- Current hash rate (2026): ~1.024 exahash/s (EH/s) — record levels
- Hash rate decline can indicate miner capitulation, potential price weakness

**Difficulty Adjustment:** Occurs every 2016 blocks to maintain ~10 min blocks.
Rising difficulty = more competition among miners = healthy network.

## On-Chain Fundamentals

| Metric | What It Measures | Bullish Signal |

| Active Addresses | Daily unique wallets transacting | 400K+ daily = healthy adoption |
| Exchange Netflow | BTC flowing in/out of exchanges | Negative (outflow) = accumulation |
| MVRV Ratio | Market cap / Realized value | < 1.0 = historically undervalued |
| SOPR | Spent Output Profit Ratio | < 1.0 = sellers at a loss, bottom zone |
| Whale Holdings | BTC held by large wallets (>1000 BTC) | Increasing = institutional accumulation |

## Bitcoin as a Trading Asset

Bitcoin is the most liquid crypto asset and serves as the market beta.
When BTC falls, most altcoins fall faster. When BTC rises, altcoins often lag then outperform.

**BTC.D (Bitcoin Dominance):** BTC market cap / total crypto market cap.
- Rising BTC.D in bear market = capital fleeing to "safety" in BTC
- Falling BTC.D in bull market = altcoin season, capital rotating to risk

## Correlation with Traditional Markets

- BTC historically correlates with NASDAQ during risk-off periods
- Low correlation with B3 (Ibovespa) — useful diversification for Brazilian portfolios
- Gold correlation strengthens during macro uncertainty
- USD strength (DXY) inversely correlated with BTC in risk-off environments

## Key Price Levels (Structural)

Technical levels to watch on Bitcoin charts:
- **All-Time High (ATH):** Strongest resistance until broken
- **Previous Cycle High:** Key psychological support in corrections
- **200-week SMA:** Historical bear market floor
- **Realized Price:** Average cost basis of all BTC on-chain
