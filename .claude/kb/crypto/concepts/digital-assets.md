# Digital Asset Types

## Asset Classification

```text
DIGITAL ASSETS
├── Layer 1 (Base chains)        BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, AVAX
├── Layer 2 (Scaling)            ARB, OP, MATIC, BASE

├── DeFi Tokens                  UNI, AAVE, LINK, MKR
```

## Layer 1 Protocols

| Asset | Network | Consensus | Use Case |
|-------|---------|-----------|----------|
| BTC | Bitcoin | PoW (SHA-256) | Digital gold, store of value |
| ETH | Ethereum | PoS (Casper) | Smart contracts, DeFi, NFTs |
| SOL | Solana | PoH + PoS | High throughput DApps |
| ADA | Cardano | PoS (Ouroboros) | Academic approach, formal verification |
| AVAX | Avalanche | PoS (Snowman) | Fast finality, subnets |

**Ethereum in 2026:** Primary settlement layer for DeFi and tokenized real-world assets (RWA).
Institutional interest expanding through custodial staking and ETH ETFs.

## Stablecoins

Stablecoins peg their value to a reference asset (usually USD). Used for:
- Parking profits without exiting crypto
- Hedging during high volatility
- Trading pairs on exchanges

| Stablecoin | Type | Backing | Risk Level |
|------------|------|---------|------------|
| USDT | Fiat-backed | Tether reserves | Medium (counterparty) |
| USDC | Fiat-backed | Circle / regulated | Low |
| DAI | Crypto-backed | ETH collateral + MKR | Medium (smart contract) |
| BUSD | Fiat-backed | Binance / Paxos | Low (regulated) |

**Brazilian note:** Stablecoins received in Brazil are taxable as foreign assets.
USDT/USDC gains must be declared and taxed at 17.5% (2026 rules).

## DeFi Tokens

Decentralized Finance (DeFi) tokens provide governance rights or protocol fees.
They are higher risk, higher potential return than BTC/ETH.

| Token | Protocol | Category | Risk |
|-------|----------|----------|------|
| UNI | Uniswap | DEX governance | High |
| AAVE | Aave | Lending protocol | High |
| LINK | Chainlink | Oracle network | Medium-High |
| MKR | MakerDAO | Stablecoin governance | High |

**Swing trading DeFi:** Higher volatility (2-5x BTC volatility is common).
Requires tighter stops and smaller position sizes.

## Exchange Tokens

| Token | Exchange | Utility |
|-------|----------|---------|
| BNB | Binance | Fee discount, Binance Chain gas |
| CRO | Crypto.com | Rewards, staking |
| OKB | OKX | Fee discount, platform access |

**BNB note:** Closely tied to Binance trading volume. Strong correlation to broader
crypto market but also to Binance-specific news (regulatory actions).

## Meme Coins (High Risk)

DOGE, SHIB, PEPE — driven by social sentiment, not fundamentals.
- Extreme volatility: 50-90% drawdowns are common
- No fundamental filter applicable
- Suitable only for speculative, small-size positions
- Not recommended for systematic screener inclusion

## B3 Crypto ETFs (Brazilian Market)

The safest way for Brazilian investors to access crypto via regulated infrastructure:

| Ticker | Asset | Benchmark | Gestora | Liquidity |
|--------|-------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| BITH11 | Bitcoin | Nasdaq BTC Reference Price | Hashdex | High |
| QBTC11 | Bitcoin | CME CF BTC Reference Rate | QR Asset | Medium |
| ETHE11 | Ethereum | Nasdaq ETH Reference Price | Hashdex | Medium |
| HASH11 | Crypto index | Nasdaq Crypto Index | Hashdex | Medium |

**ETF advantages for Brazil:**
- No need to open foreign exchange account
- Capital gains declared as renda variavel (like stocks)
- Tax treatment same as B3 stocks (swing trade 20%, day trade 20%, loss offset)
- Custody via Brazilian broker (XP, BTG, Rico, etc.)

## Tokenized Real-World Assets (RWA)

Emerging category in 2026: traditional assets represented on blockchain.
Examples: tokenized government bonds, real estate, commodities.
Not yet available on Binance spot as liquid trading pairs at scale.
