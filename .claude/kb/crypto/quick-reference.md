# Crypto Quick Reference

## RSI Thresholds (Crypto)

| RSI Value | Signal | Action |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| < 30 | Oversold | Potential long entry |
| 30-50 | Bearish momentum | Wait for reversal |
| 50-70 | Bullish momentum | Trend continuation |
| > 70 | Overbought | Potential short / take profit |

## Key EMAs for Swing Trading

| EMA | Timeframe | Use |
|-----|-----------|-----|
| EMA 8 | Short | Fast trend direction |
| EMA 21 | Medium | Swing support/resistance |
| EMA 50 | Medium | Trend filter |
| EMA 200 | Long | Bull/bear regime filter |

Golden Cross: EMA50 crosses above EMA200 → bullish regime
Death Cross: EMA50 crosses below EMA200 → bearish regime

## Bollinger Bands (20, 2)

- Price at lower band + RSI < 30 → oversold long setup
- Price at upper band + RSI > 70 → overbought short setup
- Band squeeze (low width) → breakout imminent, await direction

## Funding Rate (Futures)

- Positive funding: longs pay shorts (market bullish, expensive to be long)
- Negative funding: shorts pay longs (market bearish, expensive to be short)
- Funding settles every 8 hours on Binance perpetuals
